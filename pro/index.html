Sort Pro: just an idea

Sort Pro does not exist. We are deciding whether to build it, and your answer decides. Nothing is charged, nothing changes in the free Sort Paragraphs add-on.

What it would add to Sort Paragraphs

  • Numbers and dates in the right order (2 before 10; 3 Jan before 12 Feb).
  • Sort by the last word (surnames) or by a second key.
  • Remove duplicate lines while sorting.

Price we have in mind: 19 € once, no subscription. If we build it, the people who answered here will hear first, with a discount.
