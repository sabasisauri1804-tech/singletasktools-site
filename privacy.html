Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy covers every add-on published by Single Task Tools for Google Docs™, Google Sheets™ and Google Slides™ (the full list is on the home page, including Sort Paragraphs, Remove Line Breaks, Custom Page Size & Margins, Randomize Slides, Case Converter, Text Effects, Address to Coordinates, Spell Numbers, Statistics Tools, Amount in Words, Letter Case, Sort Table Rows, Heading Numbers, Image Resizer, Slide Index, Word Highlighter, Translation Roulette, Font Picker, Auto Documents and Rows to Calendar), and this website.

The short version

Our add-ons run inside your own Google account, on Google’s infrastructure. Your content never reaches us: we have no servers that receive it, we do not collect, store, share, sell or disclose Google user data, and we do not use it to train or improve any AI or machine-learning model.

What each add-on can access

  • The open file only. Most of our add-ons ask for a single permission, limited to the document, spreadsheet or presentation that is open when you use them (Google shows it as “View and manage documents/spreadsheets/presentations that this application has been installed in”). They read the text, cells or slides you selected, do their one task (sort, clean, resize, number, highlight, translate…) and write the result back. They cannot see your other files.
  • Font Picker shows a sidebar inside Google Docs™ or Google Slides™ (the permission “Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications”). The sidebar lists Google Fonts and applies the one you choose to the selected text. The sample text you type in the sidebar stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere. The only thing the add-on keeps is the list of fonts you used recently, stored in your own Google account’s storage for this add-on.
  • Auto Documents creates files in your Google Drive™ (the permission “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”): the template, the output folder, the documents and the PDFs it generates from your spreadsheet. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Rows to Calendar creates calendars of its own in your Google Calendar™ and manages the events in them (the permission “Make secondary Google calendars, and see, create, change and delete events on them”). It has no access to your main calendar or to calendars shared with you.
  • Translation Roulette sends the text you select to Google’s own translation service through your account, and Address to Coordinates sends the addresses you select to Google’s geocoding service through your account, so that the result comes back into your file. We do not receive, store or see that text.
  • Auto Documents and Rows to Calendar also read the email address of your Google account (“See your primary Google Account email address”) for one purpose: to check that a Pro code belongs to the account it is pasted in. The address is compared locally and never sent to us.
  • Every add-on reads the language of your Google account to show its menus in your language.

Installing an add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace includes Google’s standard sign-in, which shows the add-on your name and email address. The add-ons never store them.

What we collect

Nothing from Google. The add-ons are scripts that run on Google’s infrastructure under your account. They make no requests to servers of ours (we have none) or to any third party, we run no analytics, no advertising and no error reporting inside them, and we set no cookies through them.

If you buy a Pro plan (Auto Documents or Rows to Calendar), the payment is processed by Stripe, who acts as the seller of record. From Stripe, not from Google, we receive the email address you paid with, the date and the receipt number, so that we can send you your Pro code and answer support questions. That is the only personal data we hold.

Sharing, transfer and disclosure of Google user data

We do not share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone: not to advertisers, data brokers, partners or any other third party, and not to Stripe. We use no subprocessors. The only services that ever handle your content are Google’s own (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive, Calendar, Translate, Maps), under your account and under Google’s terms. We would disclose data only if legally required to do so, and we have never been.

How we protect data, including sensitive data

  • Your content never leaves Google’s infrastructure: there is no transfer to us, so there is nothing of yours for us to secure, lose or leak.
  • Each add-on asks for the narrowest permission that its one task needs (see the list above), and uses it only for that task. We never request access to Gmail, to your whole Drive, to your main calendar or to your contacts.
  • Sensitive content inside your files (patient names in a sheet, contract terms in a document, the text you translate) is processed only inside your account and only when you run the add-on; it is not copied, cached or logged by us.
  • Pro codes are signed with a secret key and bound to your Google account, so a code cannot be forged or used from another account. The purchase records we hold (email, date, receipt) live in a private spreadsheet in our Google Workspace account, protected by 2-step verification and accessible only to the developer; Stripe protects the payment details.
  • This website is static and served over HTTPS; it has no accounts, no databases and no tracking.

Retention and deletion

  • Google user data: we retain none, because none is transferred to us. Everything an add-on produces (sorted text, generated documents, calendar events) is yours and lives in your Google account; you delete it like any other file or event.
  • Preferences an add-on keeps for you (for example the recent fonts in Font Picker, the saved word lists in Word Highlighter, the column settings in Auto Documents and Rows to Calendar, your Pro code and the free-plan counter) are stored in your own Google account’s storage for that add-on, through Google Apps Script’s properties service. We cannot read them. They stay there until you remove the add-on or ask us to reset them, and they are never shared.
  • Purchase records (email, date, receipt) are kept for as long as your Pro plan can be used, so that we can re-send your code, and for the period Spanish tax law requires for sales records. You can ask us at any time to delete your email from our records; after that we can no longer re-send a code.
  • If you write to us, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and delete it on request.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

We do not use any Google user data, nor any data obtained through Google Workspace APIs, to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. Our add-ons contain no AI features and send nothing to AI services.

Google user data

Our add-ons’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The add-on’s page in the Marketplace

The add-on’s listing page in the Google Workspace Marketplace uses Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, so that we can see how many people visit that page and from which countries. That page belongs to Google. Our add-ons and this website do not use Google Analytics.

This website

This website is static. It uses no cookies and no tracking scripts. The company that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Email

If you write to us, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.
