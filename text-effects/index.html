Text Effects: Rainbow, Gradient & Fun Colors

This add-on colors the text you select in Google Docs™ or Google Slides™ with one click: rainbow letters, rainbow words, a smooth rainbow, a two-color gradient, random or alternating colors, a fade to gray, rainbow highlight, growing or shrinking letters, a wave of sizes, upside-down, bubble or wide letters. Colors, highlights and sizes are normal formatting in your file: they print, they export to PDF, and Ctrl+Z takes them back. It is free.

How to use it

  1. Select the words you want to color. In Google Slides™, select the text inside a box, or select one or more boxes to color all their text.
  2. Open the Extensions menu, find Text Effects and pick an effect.
  3. Not what you wanted? Press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac). Remove all effects clears colors, highlights and sizes from the selection at any time.

The effects

  • Colors: rainbow letters · rainbow words · smooth rainbow · pastel rainbow (for dark backgrounds) · gradient (six presets or your own two colors, by name or hex code) · random colors · alternating colors · fade to gray.
  • Highlight: rainbow highlight · remove highlight.
  • Size: growing · shrinking · wave · reset size.
  • Letters: upside down · bubble letters · wide letters · alternating caps. These change the characters themselves; Ctrl+Z puts them back, and the first three also go back when you apply them a second time. Accented letters and ñ stay upright when you flip text, so it comes back exactly.

The colors are chosen to stay readable on a white page: a deep amber instead of a washed-out yellow. Spaces and punctuation are skipped, so the colors land on the words. Accented letters, ñ, ç, ü and other alphabets count as letters.

Languages

The menus and messages follow the language of your Google account: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian. Any other language shows English.

Questions

How much text can I color at once?
Up to 4,000 characters per run in Google Docs™ and 1,500 in Google Slides™. For longer text, select it in parts.
Can I undo?
Yes. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) puts the previous text back. The letter effects (upside down, bubble, wide) also go back when you apply them a second time.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Docs™ and Google Slides™ add-ons run in the web version only.
What can it see?
Only the document or presentation you have open, and only while you use it. Nothing leaves your file: no servers, no tracking, no account to create. See the privacy policy.
