Remove Line Breaks: Fix Text Pasted from PDF

This add-on fixes text pasted into Google Docs™ from a PDF, an email or a web page: the lines that were cut in the middle of a sentence become normal paragraphs again. It is free.

How to use it

  1. Select the text you pasted. If you select nothing, the add-on asks before fixing the whole document.
  2. Open the Extensions menu and find Remove Line Breaks: Fix Text Pasted from PDF.
  3. Choose Join broken lines into paragraphs, Remove extra blank lines, Remove double spaces or Fix everything.

What it takes care of

  • Lines cut mid-sentence are joined into one paragraph. A blank line, a heading, a list item or a line that ends in a full stop still starts a new paragraph.
  • Words split with a hyphen at the end of a line are mended.
  • Chinese and Japanese text is joined without adding spaces.
  • Bold, italics, links and the paragraph style stay. Lists, headings, tables, images and page breaks are never touched.
  • “Remove extra blank lines” keeps one blank line between paragraphs, so the separation between your chapters stays.

Languages

The menus follow the language of your Google account: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian. Any other language shows English.

Questions

It joined two lines that should have stayed apart.
Press Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac): one press puts everything back. The add-on cannot read your mind: a short line without a full stop before a capital letter is treated as a title and kept apart; a longer one is joined.
Nothing happens when I pick an option.
The selection had nothing to fix, or it mixes areas (for example the body and a table cell). Select text inside one area.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Docs add-ons run in the web version only.
What can it see?
Only the document you have open, and only while you use it. See the privacy policy.
