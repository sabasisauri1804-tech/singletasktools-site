Translation Roulette: Lost in 20 Languages

A free add-on for Google Docs™ by Single Task Tools. One task: take the sentence you select, translate it through a chain of random languages and bring it back to yours, a little (or a lot) off. For a laugh, for language classes, for writing workshops. Menus in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

Install it from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ (search for “Translation Roulette”), or from Extensions › Add-ons › Get add-ons inside Google Docs™.

How to use it

  1. Select a sentence or a short paragraph in your document.
  2. Go to Extensions › Translation Roulette › Spin it through 10 languages (or 5, or 20, or choose how many, from 1 to 30).
  3. The result appears right below your selection, and a message shows the path it took. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes it.

Options

  • Translate back to: the language the text should come back to (the language of your Docs by default).
  • Replace the selected text instead of inserting the result below it.
  • Insert example text adds a sentence to spin, so you can try it on any document.

Worth knowing

  • Up to 1,000 characters per spin and 30 languages; a 10-language spin took under two seconds.
  • The translations come from the translation service built into Google Apps Script™; each spin uses one call per language from the daily allowance of your own Google account. The text goes to Google’s translator and comes back to your document, like Tools › Translate document; nothing is stored anywhere.

Questions

Does anything leave my document?
The selected sentence is sent to Google’s translation service, the same one behind Tools › Translate document, and comes straight back. Nothing goes to servers of ours and nothing is stored. See the privacy policy.
Why does a famous sentence come back unchanged?
Well-known sentences are translated very consistently. Try a longer, more personal one, or more languages.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Docs™ add-ons run in the web version only.
