Word Highlighter: Highlight a List of Words

A free add-on for Google Docs™ by Single Task Tools. One task: highlight, in one go, every occurrence of a list of words or phrases in your document, or count them: key terms, names, filler words, adverbs, repeated words. Menus in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

Install it from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ (search for “Word Highlighter”), or from Extensions › Add-ons › Get add-ons inside Google Docs™.

How to use it

  1. Open your document and go to Extensions › Word Highlighter › Highlight a list of words.
  2. Type the words or phrases separated by commas (for example: very, really, in order to) and press OK.
  3. Done: every occurrence is highlighted, and a message tells you how many times each entry was found and which ones were not found. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes the whole run.

Also in the menu

  • Highlight the last list again reuses the list you typed last time, in any document, and My lists keeps up to ten named lists (save the last one, highlight or delete a saved one).
  • Highlight the words of the selected text: select a line or paragraph that contains your list and the add-on uses it as the list.
  • Count a list of words: the same report, without highlighting, with the option to insert it at the end of the document.
  • Presets: filler words, adverbs, doubled words (“the the”) and the most frequent words of the text, each with its own list in six languages.
  • Options: whole words only (on by default), match case (off by default) and the color (yellow, green, blue, pink or orange).
  • Remove the highlights takes off only the colors of this add-on; the highlights you made with the Docs palette stay. Insert example text adds a paragraph full of filler words to try it on.

Worth knowing

  • Phrases work too, extra spaces between words do not matter, accented words are matched exactly, and apostrophes and hyphens join words (“don” does not match “don’t”).
  • It searches the body of the open tab (tables included), the header, the footer and the footnotes. It works in any document you can edit, yours or shared with you.
  • Up to 200 entries per list and 10,000 highlights per run (500 highlights took under a second). Run it twice with two colors and both stay; Remove the highlights takes off only the add-on’s five shades.

Questions

Does anything leave my document?
No. The add-on is a script that runs inside your Google account and only asks for permission to edit the document it is open in. No servers of ours, no tracking, no account. See the privacy policy.
Can I keep my list in the document?
Yes. Write it on a line (words separated by commas), select that line and choose Highlight the words of the selected text.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Docs™ add-ons run in the web version only.
