Font Picker: Browse, Preview & Apply Fonts

A free add-on for Google Docs™ and Google Slides™ by Single Task Tools. One task: a font preview panel (more fonts than the Font menu shows, a font manager if you like) with every font of the Google Fonts™ library, each one drawn in its own style, that applies the font you click to the selected text. Menus in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

Install it from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ (search for “Font Picker”), or from Extensions › Add-ons › Get add-ons inside Google Docs™ or Google Slides™.

How to use it

  1. Open your document or presentation and go to Extensions › Font Picker › Open the font panel.
  2. Select some text. In Slides™, select text or click a text box; Shift+click several text boxes to change them all at once (tables and groups work too).
  3. Click a font in the panel. The text takes that font; your last fonts stay under “Recent”. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes a change.

The panel

  • Over 1,800 fonts with Latin characters, each one drawn in its own style with your own sample text.
  • Search by name (try “school” or “readable”), filter by category (sans, serif, display, handwriting, mono), sort by popularity or A–Z, and type your own sample text to see every font with your words.

Worth knowing

  • It applies the font family at once; bold, italic and the other weights (Light, Black…) stay ready in the toolbar and the Font menu of Google, as always.
  • It uses the same library as the Font menu of Docs™ and Slides™, so a font applied here behaves like any other font of the document: it stays in the file, prints and exports to PDF.
  • Custom or system fonts can't be added: Docs™ and Slides™ only use the Google Fonts™ library. That is a limit of Google's editors, not of this add-on.
  • In Slides™, pictures, lines and videos are left alone; only text changes.

Questions

Does anything leave my file?
The add-on is a script that runs inside your Google account and only asks for permission to edit the file it is open in, plus permission to show its own side panel. No servers of ours, no tracking, no account. The previews are loaded straight from Google Fonts™, the same place Docs™ gets its fonts, and the sample text you type in the panel stays in your browser. See the privacy policy.
Why don't I see my computer's fonts?
Google Docs™ and Slides™ can only use fonts from the Google Fonts™ library. No add-on can add fonts installed on your computer.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Docs™ and Slides™ add-ons run in the web version only.
