Small, careful add-ons for Google Workspace™

We make single-purpose tools that do one job well, ask for the fewest permissions possible and keep your data inside your own documents.

Sort Paragraphs: Alphabetize & Shuffle

Put the paragraphs or list items you selected in alphabetical order, reverse them or shuffle them. For Google Docs™. Free.

Remove Line Breaks: Fix Text Pasted from PDF

Join the lines that a PDF or an email cut in the middle of a sentence into real paragraphs, and remove extra blank lines and double spaces. For Google Docs™. Free.

Custom Page Size & Margins

Set any page size: KDP book sizes, A5, A6, Oficio (Mexico), index cards, envelopes or your own measures in cm or inches, plus margins. For Google Docs™. Free.

Randomize Slides: Shuffle Slide Order

Put your slides in a random order with one click: all of them, all but the title slide, or only the ones you select; keep pairs together or reverse the order. For Google Slides™. Free.

Case Converter: Uppercase, Lowercase, Title Case

Change the case of the selected cells (uppercase, lowercase, capitalize each word, sentence case, title case, toggle) without touching formulas, numbers, bold or links. For Google Sheets™. Free.

Text Effects: Rainbow, Gradient & Fun Colors

Color the words you select with one click: rainbow letters, gradients, random or alternating colors, growing letters, upside-down text. For Google Docs™ and Google Slides™. Free.

Address to Coordinates: Latitude & Longitude

Write the latitude and longitude of a column of addresses, from any country, or turn coordinates into addresses. Free: 1,000 addresses a day, no API key. For Google Sheets™.

Spell Numbers: Amount in Words for Invoices

Write numbers as words, with the currency, the way each country writes it: pesos 50/100 M.N., reais e centavos, dollars and cents. By menu or with the =SPELLNUMBER formula. Six languages, ten currencies. For Google Sheets™. Free.

Statistics Tools: t-Test, ANOVA & Regression

The classic data analysis tools in Google Sheets™: descriptive statistics, correlation, regression, t-tests, F-test, ANOVA and histograms, each as a finished table in a new sheet, in six languages. Free.

Amount in Words: Spell Numbers in Contracts

Write the amount in words right after the figure, the way contracts and invoices need it: $1,500.50 (One thousand five hundred dollars and fifty cents). Six languages, ten currencies. For Google Docs™. Free.

Letter Case: Upper, Lower, Title & Sentence Case

Change the case of the selected text without losing bold, colors or links: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, tOGGLE cASE. Removes highlighting too. For Google Docs™. Free.

Sort Table Rows: A-Z, Numbers & Dates

Sort a table by any column, keeping the header row and the formatting: A to Z, Z to A, numbers or dates, from the cell you click in. For Google Docs™. Free.

Slide Index: Table of Contents with Links

A contents slide with one line per slide, each linked to its slide: click a line to jump there while presenting. One click updates it when slides change. For Google Slides™. Free.

Font Picker: Browse, Preview & Apply Fonts

Every font of the Google Fonts™ library in a side panel, each drawn in its own style: search, filter, change the sample text and click to apply it to your text. For Google Docs™ and Slides™. Free.

Heading Numbers: Auto Number Headings & Sections

Numbers every heading of your document by level (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, or 1., 1.1., or I., A., 1.), updates after changes, removes with one click. For Google Docs™. Free.

Image Resizer: Fit to Page, Same Width & Scale

Resizes all the pictures of a document at once, keeping their proportions: fit the wide ones to the page, give every picture the same width in cm, inches or pixels, or scale them by a percentage. For Google Docs™. Free.

Word Highlighter: Bulk Highlight a List of Words

Highlights, in one go, every occurrence of a list of words or phrases in your document, or counts them: key terms, names, filler words, adverbs, repeated words. For Google Docs™. Free.

Translation Roulette: Lost in 20 Languages

Takes the sentence you select, translates it through a chain of random languages and brings it back to yours, a little (or a lot) off. For a laugh, for language classes, for writing workshops. For Google Docs™. Free.

Rows to Calendar: Create Events from a Spreadsheet

For Google Sheets™. Turn the rows of a spreadsheet into Google Calendar™ events in one run, weekly repeats too, into a calendar of its own that leaves your other calendars untouched. Free up to 100 events a month; Pro 29 € once.

Auto Documents: Invoices, Contracts & PDF per Row

For Google Sheets™. Invoices, contracts, certificates, receipts and letters: one Google Docs™ file and one PDF per row, from a template with <<Column>> tags, logo included. Free up to 100 documents a month; Pro 49 € a year.
