Slide Index: Table of Contents with Links

A free add-on for Google Slides™ by Single Task Tools. One task: an automatic contents (agenda) slide with one linked line per slide, per section or per selected slide, rewritten in one go when your slides change. Menus in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

Install it from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ (search for “Slide Index”), or from Extensions › Add-ons › Get add-ons inside Google Slides™.

How to use it

  1. Open your presentation and go to Extensions › Slide Index › Insert index slide. It goes right after the slide you have selected (or after the title slide).
  2. Done: a “Contents” slide with one line per slide (“3. Title”), each one linked to its slide. In presentation mode, one click on a line jumps there; in the editor, click the line and then the link that pops up.
  3. After you add, move or rename slides, choose Update the index: numbers and links are rewritten in one go. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) undoes it.

Options

  • Index of the selected slides: select the slides you want listed in the filmstrip on the left (Ctrl+click), then choose Insert index of the selected slides. Update the index keeps that choice. An index of the selected slides lists exactly those slides, whatever the other options say.
  • Only section slides: lists just the slides whose layout is Section header or Section title and description (any layout with “section” in its name), one line per topic, so a 60-slide course gets a one-slide agenda. Off by default.
  • Slide numbers: “3. Title” or just “Title”. The number is the slide's position in the presentation, the same one you type (number + Enter) to jump to it while presenting.
  • Leave out the first slide: on by default, because the title slide is usually not listed.
  • Insert example slides adds four titled slides, so you can try it on any deck.

Worth knowing

  • The index takes the title of each slide: its title placeholder, or the first text on the slide. Slides without a title are listed as “Slide N”; skipped slides are not listed. Titles longer than about 70 characters are shortened with “…”.
  • The index slide uses the title and body layout of your theme, so it matches the rest of your deck.
  • When every slide is listed, each index slide holds 16 entries (the add-on sets the text size so they fit); longer lists continue on “Contents (2 of 3)”.
  • Update the index only rewrites the text and the links; the font and size you gave the index slide stay (the new lines take the formatting of the first line), and the lines use your theme's link color, like any link. Anything typed on it by hand is replaced.
  • The add-on recognizes its index slide by a hidden alt text on it (Slide Index 1); if you rebuild that slide by hand, just insert the index again.
  • The links survive in copies of the presentation and in the downloaded PDF (File › Download › PDF).

Questions

Does anything leave my presentation?
No. The add-on is a script that runs inside your Google account and only asks for permission to edit the presentation it is open in. No servers of ours, no tracking, no account. See the privacy policy.
Can I change how the index slide looks?
Yes: it is a normal slide, so font, size and position are yours to change, and Update the index keeps them. The lines take your theme's link color, as any link does.
Can I undo?
Yes. Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) removes the index slide (or puts the previous text back) in one step.
Does it work in the mobile app?
No. Google Slides™ add-ons run in the web version only.
