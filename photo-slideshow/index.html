Photo Slideshow: Insert Photos in Bulk

An add-on for Google Slides™ by Single Task Tools. One task: put your photos into a presentation in one go, one per slide (or 2 or 4), in order, sized to the page, with captions if you want them.

Stop inserting photos one by one. Pick them all at once in Google Drive™ or on your computer and they land on new slides after the one you are on. Install it from the Google Workspace Marketplace™ (search for “Photo Slideshow”), or from Extensions › Add-ons › Get add-ons inside Google Slides™. Menus and messages in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Italian.

How to use it

  1. Open your presentation and choose Extensions › Photo Slideshow › Insert photos.
  2. Pick the photos in Google Drive™ (My Drive, folders shared with you, shared drives: open the folder, click the first photo and Shift-click the last: the whole folder is selected in two clicks) or choose JPEG and PNG files from your computer.
  3. Choose layout, order, captions and size, then click Insert. Up to 500 photos at once: they go in a chunk at a time, with progress on screen, and you can stop at any moment.

What you can do

  • Layout: one photo per slide, fitted to the page and centered, or 2 or 4 per slide in a grid.
  • Order: as picked, by file name (IMG_2 before IMG_10) or by the date the photo was taken. Photos without a date (for example from WhatsApp) go last, in the order you picked them.
  • Captions: none, the file name, the date, or your own text typed next to each photo before inserting.
  • Size: “for screen” (a lighter copy made by Google Drive™ itself, still sharp when projected; the default) or “original” (the whole file, for printing; slower, a few seconds per photo, and it makes a heavy presentation, so keep it for a few dozen photos at most). Phone photos come in upright, and the original keeps every pixel: no recompression.
  • Big jobs: up to 500 photos at once, in chunks, with progress on screen and a Stop button. If a photo cannot be read, the rest still go in and you get the list. HEIC photos work when picked from Google Drive™; from your computer, JPEG and PNG.

Privacy

Your photos go straight from Google Drive™ or your computer into your presentation: the add-on keeps no copy, has no server of its own, and nobody but you sees them. No sign-up, no tracking. GDPR: the add-on sends us nothing, so we process no personal data of yours; only if you buy Pro do we get your email address at checkout, to send you the code, and the add-on only checks it on your side to accept the code. Details in the privacy policy.

The permissions, in plain words: it only sees the photos you select in the picker, the rest of your Drive stays out; it edits only the presentation it is open in; Google’s install screen also says “connect to an external service”: the add-on uses that permission only to download the photos you picked from Google Drive™’s servers, and nowhere else; “display web content in prompts and sidebars” is the picker and the options window; your account email is used only to tie your Pro code to your account.
